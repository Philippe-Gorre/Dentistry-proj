Everything your teeth need, in one practice

Routine care, repairs and emergencies are all handled here, so you see the same people every time.

Check-ups and cleaning

A full exam, X-rays when they are needed, and a scale and polish. Most people come every six to twelve months, depending on their teeth and gums.

Fillings and repairs

Tooth-colored fillings, crowns and bridges. We show you the problem on a screen before we start, so you know what we are fixing.

Emergency care

Toothache, a broken tooth or a lost filling. Call us and we will fit you in as soon as we can.

Whitening

Take-home trays or one session in the chair, after we check that your teeth and gums are ready for it.

Braces and aligners

Clear aligners and fixed braces for teenagers and adults, with a written plan and timeline before you commit.

Children's dentistry

Short, calm visits that let children get used to the chair and the people. Bring them as soon as their first teeth appear.

What happens at your first visit

1

We talk

We ask what brought you in and what worries you about the dentist. You set the pace from here.

2

We look

A gentle exam, with X-rays only if we need them. You see what we see, on screen, in plain language.

3

We plan

You leave with written options and their costs. Nothing is done until you say yes.
